## Website Logic Requirements

1. Display one question at a time with four selectable options.
2. Allow participants to select one answer per question.
3. Provide Next and Previous navigation buttons.
4. Show a progress indicator, such as “Question 5 of 25”.
5. Award 1 points for each correct answer.
6. Do not award points for incorrect answers.
7. After submission, display the participant's total score out of 250.
8. Display the correct answer for each question on the results screen.
9. Keep the correct answers hidden while the quiz is in progress.
10. Use a responsive, attractive college-event design suitable for desktop and mobile screens.
